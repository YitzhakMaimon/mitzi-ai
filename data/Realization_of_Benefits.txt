=== IDF DISABLED VETERANS RIGHTS - FULL EXTENDED KNOWLEDGE BASE ===

[CATEGORY: DISABILITY BRACKETS & BENEFITS BASE MATRIX]
- Bracket: 10-19% (Light Injury) | Monthly Allowance: 0 NIS | Housing Grant: 0 NIS | Medical Coverage: Only treatment for the specific recognized injuries | Rehabilitation: Not eligible for active career planning, eligible for basic medical treatments.
- Bracket: 20-29% (Basic Disability) | Monthly Allowance: 2,500 NIS | Housing Grant: 15,000 NIS | Medical Coverage: Full medical coverage for the recognized injury | Rehabilitation: Fully eligible (assigned a personal social worker, vocational testing, and tuition coverage).
- Bracket: 30-49% (Moderate Disability) | Monthly Allowance: 4,300 NIS | Housing Grant: 35,000 NIS | Medical Coverage: Full medical coverage for injury plus comprehensive dental care | Rehabilitation: Fully eligible for academic degrees and job placement.
- Bracket: 50-79% (Severe Disability) | Monthly Allowance: 7,200 NIS | Housing Grant: 75,000 NIS | Medical Coverage: Full medical coverage, specialized equipment, and financial vehicle support / mobility grants | Rehabilitation: Fully eligible.
- Bracket: 80-99% (Special Care Needed) | Monthly Allowance: 11,500 NIS | Housing Grant: 120,000 NIS | Medical Coverage: Full medical coverage and funding for a home caretaker or personal assistant | Rehabilitation: Fully eligible.
- Bracket: 100% (Total Disability) | Monthly Allowance: 15,800 NIS | Housing Grant: 200,000 NIS | Medical Coverage: Comprehensive medical coverage, car assistance, and funding for a constant companion | Rehabilitation: Fully eligible.

[CATEGORY: REHABILITATION, VOCATIONAL TESTING, AND TUITION]
According to the Rehabilitation Department policy, any veteran recognized with a disability degree of 20% and over is entitled to a full academic package. This includes 100% coverage of university or college tuition fees for a bachelor's degree. It also covers a monthly living stipend (living allowance) during the period of studies, provided the student maintains a full-time academic schedule. The department also finances professional retraining courses, vocational psychodiagnostic testing, and matches the veteran with employment coordinators to ease insertion into the modern high-tech or public job market.

[CATEGORY: EMPLOYMENT PROTECTION AND INCOME COMPLETION]
Veterans who cannot work full-time due to their medical or psychological condition are protected under special labor laws:
- Income Completion (Tashlum Tikrethe): If a veteran earns less than the minimum wage due to limited work capacity approved by a medical board, the Ministry of Defense completes the wage difference.
- Protected Workshops: Funding and placement in tailored, therapeutic workspaces for veterans requiring maximum flexibility and low-stress environments.
- Entrepreneurship Grants: One-time financial loans and business counseling for disabled veterans looking to open an independent small business.

[CATEGORY: MOBILITY AND MEDICAL VEHICLE ELIGIBILITY]
Veterans with a disability rating of 35% or higher due to mobility or leg injuries, or 50% and higher for any overall medical injury, are entitled to a medical vehicle grant (Ma'anak Rechev). 
- Every 4 to 5 years, the Ministry of Defense assists in replacing the vehicle, covering up to 60%-80% of the customs and purchase taxes.
- Monthly mobility allowances (Dmei Niyadut) are paid automatically as part of the monthly stipend to cover fuel costs, insurance premiums, and ongoing car maintenance.
- Special adaptations, such as hand-controls or wheelchair ramps, are fully funded based on the decisions of the Institute for Medical Road Safety (MARVAD).

[CATEGORY: HOUSING GRANTS, MORTGAGES, AND ARNONA REDUCTIONS]
Disabled veterans are entitled to substantial financial assistance when purchasing their first apartment or home:
- Mortgage Subsidies: State-guaranteed low-interest mortgages with special terms through designated banks.
- Rental Assistance: Monthly rent subsidies for veterans holding 20% disability or higher who do not own property, paid for a maximum of 3 to 5 years during rehabilitation.
- Arnona (Property Tax) Discount: Local municipalities grant an automatic reduction of up to 66% on property taxes for apartments up to 100 square meters. Eligible criteria start from a 10% disability rating for ancient rules, and strictly 20% or higher for modern regulations.

[CATEGORY: INITIAL APARTMENT EQUIPEMENT AND HOME ADAPTATION]
When moving into a newly purchased or rented ground floor apartment, severely disabled veterans (disabled at 50% and above, or facing severe mobility limitations) can claim:
- Renovation Budget: Funding for physical accessibility changes inside the home, including widening doors, building accessible showers, and installing ramps or stairlifts.
- Initial Equipment Grant: Financial assistance to buy primary electrical appliances like a refrigerator, washing machine, and heating/cooling systems necessary for optimal health maintenance.

[CATEGORY: POST-TRAUMATIC STRESS DISORDER (PTSD) & MENTAL HEALTH]
Veterans recognized with PTSD or combat-related psychological trauma are entitled to an immediate and holistic mental health envelope:
- Alternative Therapies: Funding for hydrotherapy, equine-assisted therapy (horseback riding), dog training therapy, and medical cannabis licenses under strict medical supervision.
- Crisis Intervention: 24/7 access to immediate psychological support lines, emergency psychiatric evaluations, and specialized retreat centers (such as 'BaBayit' projects).
- Group Therapy: Fully funded support groups for both the veterans and their direct spouses or partners to process secondary traumatization.
- Chronic Anxiety & Social Re-integration: Funding for emotional support mentors who accompany the veteran to public spaces to help overcome severe social anxieties and agoraphobia.

[CATEGORY: HOSPITALIZATION, CONVALESCENCE LEAVE, AND MEDICAL SPAS]
Under Directive 84.04 of the Disabled Rehabilitation Department, the family of a hospitalized injured veteran is entitled to immediate financial assistance. This includes full or partial reimbursement of travel expenses to the hospital, economic expenses, hotel stays near the medical center, babysitter or caretaker expenses for children at home, and financial compensation for lost workdays for one primary family member. Furthermore, every recognized veteran with a 20% rating or higher is entitled to an annual Convalescence Leave (Dmei Havra'a), funding 5 to 10 days of vacation or rest in approved hotels across Israel.
- Hot Springs & Medical Spas (Chamei Marpe): Veterans with specific orthopedic, skin, or severe internal illnesses receive annual vouchers for 7 to 14 days of fully covered clinical treatments at the Dead Sea or Tiberias hot springs.

[CATEGORY: MEDICAL PENSIONS AND CHRONIC ILLNESS LAW]
Veterans whose injuries prevent them from working entirely receive a long-term safety net:
- Permanent Medical Pension (Tagmul Kiyum): Paid monthly to veterans recognized with 50% or higher disability who are declared permanently unfit for any work by a specialized occupational committee.
- Retroactive Escalation (Va'ada Chozereth): Veterans can request a medical re-evaluation every 6 months if their physical or mental health status deteriorates, allowing them to upgrade their disability percentage brackets retroactively.

[CATEGORY: LEGAL AID AND REPRESENTATION RIGHT]
The Rehabilitation Department funds free legal representation and consultations for veterans going through complex bureaucratic processes:
- Medical Boards Representation: Pro-bono lawyers specialized in defense laws assist veterans during appeals at the Supreme Medical Committee (Va'ada Re'fuit Elyona).
- General Rights Exhaustion Representation: Free legal assistance to fight municipal errors regarding Arnona, income tax reductions, or rental disputes.

[CATEGORY: INCOME TAX EXEMPTIONS AND CAPITAL REVENUE RIGHTS]
- Bracket: 10-19% (Light Injury) | Income Tax Reduction: 0% | Capital Gains: No exemption | Customs Benefit: No reduction.
- Bracket: 20-49% (Moderate Disability) | Income Tax Reduction: Partial points reduction (Nekudot Zichuy) | Capital Gains: No exemption | Customs Benefit: Minor tax exemptions on specific medical imports.
- Bracket: 50-89% (Severe Disability) | Income Tax Reduction: Comprehensive tax reduction on salary income up to specified legal ceilings | Capital Gains: Partial exemption on real estate appreciation | Customs Benefit: Substantial tax cuts on import of vital lifestyle assistance products.
- Bracket: 90-100% (Total Disability) | Income Tax Reduction: Complete 100% exemption from personal income tax (Mas Hachnasa) on active and passive income up to the maximum legal limit | Capital Gains: Full exemption on selected investments | Customs Benefit: Complete waiver of purchase taxes on medical apparatus and essential mobility systems.

[CATEGORY: HIGHER EDUCATION FOR CHILDREN AND FAMILY TUITION SUBSIDIES]
According to the Welfare and Benefits Division regulations, children of severely disabled veterans (recognized with a disability degree of 50% and over) are entitled to comprehensive educational sponsorship. This package includes full funding of 100% of academic tuition fees for a bachelor's degree or professional engineering diploma at recognized public universities and academic colleges. The department also provides yearly textbook grants, automated registration fee reimbursements, and targeted financial support for learning disability assessments (Achuva Testing). For veterans holding 100% disability, these academic benefits are fully extended to include the spouse or legal partner for career retraining programs.

[CATEGORY: STRATEGIC ARMED FORCES INSURANCE AND SPECIAL DEATH BENEFITS]
Disabled veterans holding a rating of 50% or higher are automatically covered by a state-funded mutual insurance framework:
- Life Insurance Framework (Bituach Chayim): The Ministry of Defense finances the monthly premiums for comprehensive life insurance policies, ensuring financial stability for dependents.
- Funeral and Burial Coverage: Full funding of all state burial arrangements, plots in designated civilian sectors, and immediate emotional support grants for mourning family members.
- Transitional Spouse Stipend: In the event of a veteran's passing due to complications of recognized injuries, the primary spouse transitions smoothly to receive a monthly survivor pension (Tagmul She'erim).

[CATEGORY: ALTERNATIVE WHEELCHAIR PROCUREMENT AND HOME MOBILITY ACCESSIBILITY]
Veterans suffering from paralysis, amputations, or progressive neurological conditions recognized at 80% disability and above receive absolute equipment backing:
- Multi-Terrain Wheelchairs: Funding for both standard lightweight titanium wheelchairs for daily indoor use and robust off-road motorized systems for outdoor environments.
- Smart Home Integration: Full budgetary coverage for voice-activated doors, automated environmental control units (ECU), and specialized ceiling hoist systems (Manoach) for safe transfers.
- Technical Maintenance Envelope: Immediate 24/7 technical repair service for all mobility devices, including free temporary replacement units during mechanical failures.

[CATEGORY: COGNITIVE AGING REHABILITATION AND GERIATRIC CARE AMENDMENTS]
As disabled veterans age, the interface between their recognized military injuries and natural chronological aging is protected under specific geriatric clinical directives:
- Dual-Diagnosis Senior Support: Automatic expansion of monthly care hours (Sha'ot Si'ud) when a veteran reaches retirement age, bypassing standard National Insurance (Bituach Leumi) caps.
- Specialized Nursing Facility Funding: Substantial institutional subsidies for veterans requiring placement in high-tier nursing homes or geriatric medical centers due to deterioration of recognized injuries.
- Chronic Pain Management: Annual vouchers for advanced multidisciplinary pain clinics, specialized hydrotherapy protocols, and innovative orthopedic bed allocations.

[CATEGORY: MEDICAL APPARATUS PROVISION AND LONG-TERM CONSUMABLE SUPPLIES]
The Medical Supply Unit provides direct physical delivery of necessary healthcare consumables to the veteran's doorstep:
- Respiratory Equipment: Fully funded oxygen concentrators, CPAP machines, and replacement filters for veterans with recognized pulmonary or inhalation injuries.
- Orthopedic Aids: Custom-molded orthotic insoles, high-durability prosthetic limbs with computerized joints, and ergonomic back supports replaced automatically every 2 to 3 years.
- Daily Consumables: Ongoing monthly allocation of wound-care dressings, compression garments, and specialized dermatological creams for burn victims.

[CATEGORY: DENTAL COHESION AND MAXILLOFACIAL RECONSTRUCTION REGULATION]
Comprehensive dental coverage varies strictly based on disability classification and injury origin:
- Injury-Specific Dental Care: Full funding for any dental or maxillofacial treatment required as a direct result of face, jaw, or head wounds sustained during operational activity.
- General Dental Envelope: Veterans with an overall rating of 50% or higher for any medical cause receive comprehensive preventative, restorative, and periodontal dental care at approved clinics.
- Advanced Prosthodontics: Full state coverage for complex dental implants, crowns, and oral surgeries required to maintain basic nutritional health and digestive functions.

[CATEGORY: UTILITY DISCOUNTS AND HOUSEHOLD SUBSIDIES]
- Water Consumption Subsidy: Veterans holding a 50% disability rating or higher are entitled to an automatic 50% discount on the first 3.5 cubic meters of water consumed per month.
- Electricity Bill Reduction: An automatic reduction of 50% on the basic consumption component of the bi-monthly electricity bill is granted to veterans with 50% disability or higher.
- Phone Line Maintenance: National telecommunication providers grant discounted monthly base rates and call packages for any veteran holding a 20% rating or higher.

[CATEGORY: MARRIAGE AND MAJOR LIFE EVENTS EXPENDITURE]
The Welfare Division provides financial backing to ease social reintegration during significant personal milestones:
- Marriage Grant (Ma'anak Nisuin): A one-time financial gift given to disabled veterans with a 20% rating or higher when registering their first marriage legal bond.
- Wedding Adaptation Support: For severely disabled veterans (100% rating), the ministry finances unique accessibility logistical demands for the wedding hall and event transportation.
- Divorce Legal Counseling: Subsidized emotional and legal mediation services for veterans facing marital dissolution tied directly to active PTSD developments.

[CATEGORY: RECREATIONAL, SPORTING, AND BEIT HALOCHEM ACCOMMODATION]
The Ministry of Defense tightly integrates physical rehabilitation with community sport infrastructure through specialized centers:
- Beit Halochem Membership: Full state financing of annual membership fees for the veteran, their spouse, and dependent children at dedicated rehabilitation sport clubs.
- Hydrotherapy and Para-sports: Full coverage for professional coaching, adaptive sports gear, wheelchair basketball leagues, and competitive international paralympic training support.
- Cultural Event Subsidies: Allocation of highly discounted seasonal tickets for theaters, museums, and national parks to encourage active outdoor exposure.
